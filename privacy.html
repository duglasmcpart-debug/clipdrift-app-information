ClipDrift Local
Privacy Terms

Privacy policy

ClipDrift Local uses YouTube API Services. Velmur Design operates the desktop installation and handles questions about its use of Google data.

Data ClipDrift uses

When you connect YouTube, ClipDrift uses the authorized channel’s ID, title and handle to select the right channel. It uses upload receipts, video IDs, visibility and processing status to show upload results and avoid duplicate uploads. The OAuth grant lets ClipDrift upload videos and read the channel or upload status.

Where data is processed and shared

ClipDrift keeps authorization credentials on the operator’s Windows computer and protects them with Windows data protection. Channel information, upload records, local media and processing logs are stored in the local installation. Whisper and Ollama process media and text on that computer. For an authorized upload, ClipDrift sends the video and related metadata to YouTube. YouTube handles the video and its visibility under the Google Privacy Policy.

The public documentation site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Its host may process basic connection data to deliver the pages. ClipDrift’s YouTube credentials and clips are kept outside the documentation site.

Access, revocation and deletion

Use Disconnect in ClipDrift to remove the active connection. You can also revoke access in Google Account permissions. For removal of stored app data, including local records and copies, or to ask a privacy question, contact velmurdesign@gmail.com. You can manage uploaded videos in YouTube Studio.
